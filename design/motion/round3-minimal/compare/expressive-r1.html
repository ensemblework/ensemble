Ensemble · motion system

Loaders & working animations

Built on two voices: violet is the agent, paper is you. Everything here is plain CSS and SVG, plus a small glyph engine in vanilla JS. Every animation has a reduced-motion version. Try the toggle.
01

Signature: the Ensemble Glyph

Where: the assistant dock header, the Ask button's Mark, activity pill, splash, toasts, job rows (at 14–16 px).
Idea: the icon's two strands never stop being two strands. While the agent works they morph mark → braid (weaving, with crossings that travel) → linked rings → spark (spinning ✦, our answer to Claude's asterisk) → merge (one ring: in sync) → mark. States morph from wherever they are: wait, pause, done, error.

02

App boot splash

Where: first paint, before the shell query resolves. Also the native app's launch window on Mac, Windows and Linux.
Idea: the icon draws itself. The violet strand runs first with a bead leading it, then your strand follows it in. The wordmark rises and the status line takes over ("Tuning in…") until the shell is ready, then the splash lifts away.

11

Kill switch · stop · error

Where: ActivityControls (Pause/Resume, Stop everything), the assistant's Stop, failed runs, and error toasts.
Idea: on Pause the glyph's two strands straighten into pause bars. The icon becomes the control. Everything that was moving freezes and greys out mid-stitch. Resume bends the strands back into the mark. On an error the strands fray and turn red.
