Rehearse first, then record once.

1. Find the real path before you record: open the site or app, find the fields and buttons, and note the steps. Deal with sign-ins and pop-ups first.
2. Record only the steps asked for, at a pace a person can follow, with nothing private on screen (no passwords, no unrelated tabs or mail).
3. Keep one recording at a time. Stop and save it cleanly; discard a failed take rather than sending it.
4. Check the file before you deliver it: it plays, it shows the task, and its length is what you expect.
5. It comes with your reply when the tool made it; say in one line what it shows.
