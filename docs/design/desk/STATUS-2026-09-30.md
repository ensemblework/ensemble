## Integration answer

Desk tiles plug into the existing model well enough to keep building. A backend reshape is not the next step.

Today's live payload reads `Task`, `Project`, `Person`, `Deliverable`, `Reminder`, `Artifact`, `MeetingNote`, and `Repo`, plus a small set of side tables added in `20260930220000_desk_fields` (timetable slots, court holidays, attendance, objectives, deploys, parts, tests, citations, grading). Those tables are owned by `userId`. Limitation dates are computed in `apps/hub-api/src/desk/limitation.ts`, not stored as a second due date.

The debt is two drawings of one desk. Sample mode (`desk.sample`) renders the mockup boards in `components/desk/boards` and `components/desk/widgets`. Live mode renders `components/desk/live-board.tsx`: a numeral and up to five rows. Both are fed, when live, by one `GET /api/desk/live`. Unifying those drawings does not require new tables.

Diagrams are already their own module, on for every persona desk, separate from Code. Today shows a server-rendered SVG strip when the account has diagrams. They are not a bento tile. Step 2 added them as Context graph nodes; the screenshot review draws their names and opens the editor from a node.
